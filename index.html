About

About Miami Metro LLC

Miami Metro LLC is a company headquartered in Miami, Florida. It builds and runs digital products, including LeadCove, BenefitBoost, AIFeed.run, and Mirapop, and it operates local sites such as 305Electricians, 305Plumbers, and Salty Fresh.

What is Miami Metro LLC?

Miami Metro LLC is a Miami-based studio that designs, ships, and operates its own digital products and local service sites. For questions, email hello@miamimetro.us. This website does not publish a street address.

Do Miami Metro, 305Electricians, or 305Plumbers perform electrical or plumbing work?

No. Miami Metro LLC is not a contractor. 305Electricians and 305Plumbers are referral services. They connect the customer with an independent licensed professional, and that professional does the work.

How does a licensed electrician or plumber join?

Electricians can apply at 305electricians.com/pros/. Plumbers can apply at 305plumbers.com/pros/ or email pros@305plumbers.com.

Is BenefitBoost a government website?

No. BenefitBoost is a free website run by Miami Metro LLC. It is not a government agency and it is not affiliated with USDA or SNAP. It never asks for an EBT card number or PIN.

How can I contact Miami Metro LLC?

Email hello@miamimetro.us. We are headquartered in Miami, Florida.
